# A-10C II – cold start sequence

Cold-start sequence taken from the Aircraft Startup Procedures chapter of the DCS A-10C II flight manual (pp. 504–545, see [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md)), written in our own words. Page numbers are the manual's pages; controls are named as in [PANELS.md](PANELS.md), and a control missing there is flagged in its Control cell. Rows are numbered continuously through the file.

## Automatic start

Page 504 says an automatic start also exists, but the chapter gives no key combination or steps for it: not stated. The Throttles section of PANELS.md notes that moving the throttle past the hump starts the engine's auto-start (manual p. 111).
